Eczema severity, classified in seconds.

DermaScan runs two models — a custom CNN and a transfer-learned MobileNetV2 — side by side, then uses Grad-CAM to show exactly where each model looked. Upload a skin image to get a high / low severity verdict.

On mobile, "Take a photo" opens your camera directly. On desktop, it opens the file picker.

How it works

🧠

Two models, one verdict

A custom CNN and MobileNetV2 each predict independently. When they agree, you can trust the call more.

🔥

Grad-CAM explainability

A jet-colormap heatmap blended over your image reveals which regions drove the prediction.

⚡

In-memory & private

Images are validated, classified, and discarded. Nothing is written to disk — no personal data retained.
